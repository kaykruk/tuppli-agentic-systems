# 🗺️ Tuppli Strategic Roadmap
*Synthesized from the "Domain Knowledge" and "$100k/month Growth" Success Playbooks.*

---

## 🎯 Phase 1: Deep Friction Audit (The "Roadmap Mine")
**Objective**: Build the product roadmap based on the frustrations of competitors' customers.
- [ ] **Reddit & FB Group Analysis**: Execute `reddit_scraper.js` to mine 500+ posts in `OnlyFansAdvice` and `CreatorsAdvice`.
- [ ] **Pain Mapping**: Identify 3-5 specific "insider" pain points (e.g., "manual takedown delays", "fake Reddit accounts stealing traffic").
- [ ] **Clone and Conquer**: Audit top 5 competitors (Rentity, BranditScan, etc.) and identify exactly where they are failing their users.

## 🏗️ Phase 2: The 72-Hour ROI Sprint
**Objective**: Build a functional, non-perfect version of the highest-ROI feature.
- [ ] **"Free Scan" Enhancement**: Focus on the single feature with the highest conversion (e.g., "One-Click Impersonator Audit").
- [ ] **Cursor-Driven Build**: Break the build into tiny, manageable steps using Cursor for logic and n8n for automation.
- [ ] **Ship Daily**: Establish an iterative loop where one improvement is shipped every 24 hours.

## 🌪️ Phase 4: Viral Ubiquity
**Objective**: Turn Every User into a Distribution Node.
- [ ] **Viral Loops**: Implement the "Shareable Scorecard" in the Free Scan tool.
- [ ] **Directory Blitz**: Submit to 20+ AI and SaaS directories for backlink authority.
- [ ] **Research Lab**: Publish the weekly "Creator Security Report" powered by recon data.

---

## 🔑 Success Principles
- **Entertainment > Information**: Ads/Content must be fun to watch.
- **Utility > Marketing**: Solving a problem for free (Metadata/Search) builds the best brand.
- **Velocity > Perfection**: Launch the viral hooks immediately.
