## 🏗️ Core Educational Themes

### 1. The "Metadata Trap" (Security Hack)
**The Value**: Teaching creators how to wipe EXIF data from their photos and videos before uploading.
**The "Aha" moment**: Seeing how a simple photo can leak your GPS coordinates or device ID to a stalker.
**Call to action**: "We built Tuppli's uploader to do this automatically, but here is how you do it manually for free."

### 2. The "Telegram Honeypot" (How Leaks Happen)
**The Value**: Explaining the mechanics of how bots scrape "preview" channels to lure people into paid pirate groups.
**The "Aha" moment**: Realizing that sharing a 5-second "free" clip is often the direct catalyst for a full leak.
**Call to action**: "Understand the funnel of piracy. Stay safe, even if you don't use Tuppli."

### 3. "Watermark Physics" (Authority)
**The Value**: Explaining why static watermarks are easily stripped by AI and what "Forensic Watermarking" actually means.
**The "Aha" moment**: Understanding that a visible logo is just a suggestion to a thief, not a barrier.
**Call to action**: "Learn the difference between 'Visible' and 'Forensic' protection."
