# 🦅 Tuppli: Social Distribution System (Balanced)
*A systematic engine to build authority and trust through value-first content.*

---

## 🏗️ The 4 Content Pillars
To avoid being "the brand that only talks about others," we use a balanced pillar system.

| Pillar | Focus | Frequency | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Value & Education** | Digital safety hacks, DMCA secrets, how leaks happen. | 40% | Build trust & reciprocity. |
| **2. Authority & Insights** | "Why Telegram is the new frontline," detection speed data. | 30% | Establish technical expertise. |
| **3. Transformation** | "6 hours of work → 2 mins," creator peace of mind. | 20% | Show the emotional benefit. |
| **4. Strategic Edge** | Why general tools miss leaks (Educational comparison). | 10% | Highlight Tuppli's moat. |

---

## 2. X (Twitter) System: "The Authority Bot"
**Strategy**: High-frequency, authority-building, and reactive.
- **System**: Daily automated tweet using browser cookies.
- **Rotation**: 
  - **The Hook** (PAS): Pain-driven callouts of leaks.
  - **The Proof** (Stats): Industry piracy data.
  - **The Thread** (Value): How-to protect your brand.
- **Automation**: `scripts/post_tweet_2.js` (refactored to accept dynamic content).

---

## 3. Reddit System: "The Specialist Guard"
**Strategy**: Community-led, high-value, and non-spammy.
- **System**: Weekly "Deep Friction" posts in creator subreddits.
- **Subreddits**: `OnlyFansAdvice`, `CreatorsAdvice`, `Fansly`.
- **Engagement Rule**: Never link directly in the post. Provide value first, then invite to "DM for the private audit tool."
- **Automation**: Semi-automated drafting (I provide the title/body, you post via your active Reddit persona).

## 🛡️ Ethical Guardrails
- **No Spam**: Max 1 post per day on X, 2 posts per week on Reddit.
- **Value First**: Every post must educate the creator even if they don't buy.
- **Transparency**: Disclose Tuppli as a tool we built to solve our own problems.
