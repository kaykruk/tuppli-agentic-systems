## Summary
The LLM Wiki is a shift from traditional RAG. Instead of retrieving fragments at query time, an LLM agent incrementally builds and maintains a persistent, interlinked collection of markdown files. This "Second Brain" accumulates knowledge over time, resolving contradictions and strengthening synthesis with every new source.
