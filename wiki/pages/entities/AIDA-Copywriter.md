# Entity: AIDA Copywriter

The **AIDA Copywriter** is an AI agent designed to generate high-conversion marketing copy for Tuppli users and internal marketing.

## Technical Details
- **Implementation**: `frontend/lib/agents/copywriter.ts`
- **Model**: Gemini 1.5 Flash
- **Framework**: **AIDA** (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)

## Methodology
- **Persona**: An expert Direct Response Copywriter ("David Ogilvy" style).
- **Output**: Returns both a structured breakdown of the AIDA steps and a final polished "ready-to-publish" copy block.

## Role in Tuppli
Enables creators to quickly generate promotional text, email blasts, and landing page copy that aligns with the protection services they are using.
