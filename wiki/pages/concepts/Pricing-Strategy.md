# Concept: Pricing Strategy

Tuppli's pricing is built around a core principle: **we are not selling a product — we are protecting a source of income.** Every dollar spent on Tuppli should feel like insurance, not an expense.

## Tiers (Settled April 2026)

| Tier | Price | Audience | Key Differentiator |
|------|-------|----------|--------------------|
| **Pro** | $249.99/mo | Independent creators | Surface + social scanning, DMCA automation |
| **Elite** | $449.99/mo | High-value creators | Deep web, Telegram, forensic watermarking, court-ready evidence |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Management companies with rosters | White-label, unlimited creators, dedicated legal engineer |

## Messaging Framework
- **Don't sell.** Frame protection as saving money they would otherwise lose.
- **Exclusivity = Income.** The core value proposition is that a creator's content is only valuable if it's exclusive.
- **One leak > One year of Tuppli.** A single viral leak can cost $5,000–$15,000/month in lost revenue. Tuppli pays for itself immediately.
