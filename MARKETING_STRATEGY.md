## 🔑 Key Principles

1. **Lead with empathy, not features** — creators are frustrated, not shopping
2. **Show, don't tell** — every demo video > every tweet about features
3. **Be authentic** — founder-led content outperforms polished brand content 5:1
4. **Speed wins** — respond to leak incidents within 1 hour for maximum engagement
5. **Track everything** — use Plausible for web, Twitter Analytics, TikTok Analytics
6. **Iterate weekly** — double down on what works, kill what doesn't
