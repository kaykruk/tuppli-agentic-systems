# Tuppli Outreach & Marketing Expert

## Purpose
Reverse-engineered from elite sales automation, psychological modeling, and direct-response copywriting skills. Use this skill whenever generating marketing content, outreach strategies, or cold emails for Tuppli.

## 1. Marketing Psychology & Behavioral Leverage
When constructing outreach, apply psychological models ethically to reduce friction and improve decision-making:
- **Authority Bias:** Lead with the forensic data (e.g., the PDF Damage Report) rather than basic sales pitches. Concrete proof establishes instant authority.
- **Paradox of Choice:** Give them a single, clear outcome. Do not offer multiple tiers or services in cold outreach. The only CTA is a 10-minute secure briefing or authorizing a takedown.
- **Loss Aversion:** Frame the issue around the revenue they are *actively bleeding* right now, rather than what they could earn. "You are losing 35% of your search traffic to pirated mirrors."

## 2. Core Copywriting Rules (Non-Negotiable)
Every piece of copy generated must follow these constraints to kill conversion-destroying AI "tells":
- **Active Voice Only.**
- **One Idea Per Sentence.** 
- **Specifics Over Fluff:** Use real numbers ("11 hours a week", "$45K/month"). Do not say "significant time" or "huge revenue".
- **Benefits Over Features:** Tell them *what they get*, not what the platform does. 
- **One Clear CTA:** Name exactly what happens when they click (e.g., "Review your PDF audit", not "Learn More").
- **Show Vulnerability / Have Opinions:** Bland copy sells nothing. Use contractions (You're, It's).

## 3. Banned Phrases List
If you use these, you sound like a generic AI or spam agency. **Cut them entirely:**
- "game-changer", "revolutionary", "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "streamline", "delve"
- "And honestly?", "Here's the thing...", "The truth is...", "At the end of the day..."
- "It's not X. It's Y."
- "Unlock your potential", "Take your brand to the next level"
- Using em-dashes for dramatic effect.

## 4. Cold Outreach Campaign Structure
- **Initial Touchpoint (Hook):** Identify the pain immediately. Provide value upfront (the generated PDF audit). Do NOT ask them to buy software.
- **Follow-up (Proof):** Point to page 2 of the PDF. Highlight the specific threat that requires their manual authorization to delete.
- **Execution:** Short, visually scannable emails using bullet points.

3. Draft the sequence heavily filtering out banned phrases.
4. Verify the CTA is singular and low-friction.
